Teacher notes

Use 2 or 3 spins for a 5-minute warm-up. Start by asking Maria for today's date. Every card is a real chat: she picks a button or gives her own answer, says a whole sentence, then you ask one question from «What else?». Wait for her to build the sentence before helping. Five cards carry the test's oral questions in everyday form, so you can switch into the examiner on any of them: My family (introduce your family), Languages (why join the section), Countries, Films and TV (the last film you watched) and Halloween (English-speaking culture). My room keeps «There are lots of...» for the test writing. My family mixes looks like, interested in and right now; Free time mixes how often and always / usually / sometimes / never; Holidays mixes the past and present continuous for plans. Recast her French-way forms in the chat when they come up (her list is in her CLAUDE.md), then let her say the sentence again herself. A personal answer does not have to match a button. Invite her to ask you a question too.
