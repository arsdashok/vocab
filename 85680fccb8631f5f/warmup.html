🌀
Spin to start! 👉
Choose an idea or give your own. Say a sentence, then ask Dasha!
Teacher notes

Use 2 or 3 spins for a 5-minute warm-up. Start by asking Maria for today’s date. Offer a choice, then wait for her own sentence. Keep the sentence starters visible. Reveal the next questions only when she is ready. If she needs a verb, offer two possibilities and let her build the sentence. A personal answer does not have to match a button. Invite her to ask you a question too.
