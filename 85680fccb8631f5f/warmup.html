🐉 Maria's Warm-Up

Spin, choose and chat!
🌀
Spin to start! 👉
Choose an idea or give your own. Say a sentence, then ask me!
Teacher notes

Use 2 or 3 spins for a 5-minute warm-up. Start by asking Maria for today's date. Every card is a real chat: she picks a button or gives her own answer, says a whole sentence, then you ask one question from «What else?». Wait for her to build the sentence before helping. Two cards carry the test's oral questions in everyday form, so you can switch into the examiner on them: My family (introduce your family) and Languages (why join the section; «one more language» sits on a starter). My room keeps «There are lots of...» for the test writing. My family has a hair starter with the size word before the gap (short / long ... hair), for the adjective order from 4 November. Food comes from 6 November (her almond croissant, and éclair means lightning in French): let her explain French words in English. Its «It's...» starter is a chance to hear «it» for a thing. Wild places comes from the magazine (arctic animals on 4 November, Peru and llamas on 6 November). London stays in the present («My favourite place is...», «Next time I'd like to see...») because the past simple has not been taught. My family, Free time and My dog mix the present simple (always, usually, doesn't) with right now. No card asks her to make why-questions about a person: keep that for the picture task in the lesson. Recast her French-way forms in the chat when they come up, then let her say the sentence again herself. A personal answer does not have to match a button. Invite her to ask you a question too.
