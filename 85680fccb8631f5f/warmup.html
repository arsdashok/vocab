Teacher notes

Use 2 or 3 spins for a 5-minute warm-up. Start by asking Maria for today’s date. Five topics are the oral questions of the Collège Sylvain Menu test (About me, Why join?, Countries, Culture, Last film): play the examiner, wait for her answer, then ask one follow-up from «What else?». Right now, Interests and English way practise the French-style forms from her lessons and homework (has … years old, in this moment, one language more, interested with / by, they original, to can, other persons). Keep the sentence starters visible. «Help 💡» shows the correct version: open it only after she has tried, and let her say the whole sentence again herself. In English way, «at the moment» repairs her «in this moment»; «right now» is also fine. If mechanic or favourite comes up, ask her to spell it aloud. A personal answer does not have to match a button. Invite her to ask you a question too.
